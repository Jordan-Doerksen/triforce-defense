# Triforce-Defense — rules for every session

Read `docs/HANDOFF.md`, `DECISIONS.md` and `design/index.html` first. The owner is Jordan; blunt, no cheerleading; one question at a time with 2–4 concrete options, the recommended one first; push back on ideas, never yes-man; DECISIONS.md is written only after the owner says yes.

## The style audit rule (owner, 2026-10-17)
A change to **The look** (DECISIONS.md) is a change to every drawn or styled surface, not to the one that prompted it. When the look changes, or a new visual vocabulary is introduced:
1. Open `design/INVENTORY.md` and audit **every** row against the new look before the change is called done.
2. Restyle every row that fails. Do not patch only the surface that was noticed.
3. Record the audit in `records/style-audit-<date>.md`: the rows, pass or fail, what was done.
4. Add any new surface to `design/INVENTORY.md` when it is built.
Surgical, one-surface fixes after a style change are a defect, not progress.

## Drawing rule
Every verdict that can be drawn gets a diorama in the locked ink: flat shapes, thick black lines, stepped motion, no symmetry for its own sake. One drawing, rendered twice, beats two drawings.
