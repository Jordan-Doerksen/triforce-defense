Plate II · The Clock — three ways to draw it

Pick one. It becomes the plate's one visual, in the same skin as Plate I. All three are static, and every number is a default (the night count and the day length are still open). Council record: records/council-2026-10-16-plate-2-clock.md.

Option A

One day, one night

from council-systems-pacing and council-skeptic

Shows
One cycle at the default proportions, where the push and the wave fall, the dusk ramp, and the four strength states with the cap.
Leaves to the text
The twelve-night count and the map.
Option B

Twelve nights

from council-spark

Shows
The whole match: twelve nights, the longer first day, the relief at the last dawn, the nights-left line, and the rituals' partial credit on the ground.
Leaves to the text
The strength states, the cap and the dusk ramp; the map.
Option C

By day and by night

from council-player-guide and council-level-architect

Shows
Plate I's graph twice: by day the short roads are push routes to the rituals; by night they are wave lanes, and only the safe roads (over the bridge, through the thin woods) are roads. The push window, as the map.
Leaves to the text
The strength states and the cap; the twelve-night count.
