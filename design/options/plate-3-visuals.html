Plate III · The Two Armies — three ways to draw it

Pick one. It becomes the plate's one visual, in the same skin as Plates I and II. All three are static and print no tuning numbers. Council record: records/council-2026-10-16-plate-3-two-armies.md.

Option A

The gate, home and away

from council-spark, -systems-pacing, -skeptic and -player-guide

Shows
The plate's one lesson: when the field force leaves, the gate is held by men the captain cannot command, and they pay for the time they buy; when they are nearly gone, the horn sounds. Garrison and field force told apart by colour.
Leaves to the text
The commanders' kits; the Barracks tiers; the other two towns.
Option B

Three towns, dusk and night

from council-level-architect

Shows
The two armies across the province: a hard night leaves a gate thin at the next dusk, the valley's Knights ride the safe road to stand with it, and their own gate is left to its garrison. Continues Plate II's picture.
Leaves to the text
The commanders and their kits; the Barracks tiers.

Commanders and the ladder
