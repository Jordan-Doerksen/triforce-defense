# Pacing research — mandatory titles before the clock is re-sealed

The council found the clock does not fit its contents (13 nights in ~40 minutes is ~3 minutes a cycle). The owner: "3 minutes is way too fast; I honestly don't know the answer." These titles are read before Decision 1.4's day length is set. Add findings here; cite the source; mark what is from memory.

## Warcraft III's own clock (verified)
- One full cycle is **480 game seconds: 240 of day (06:00–18:00), 240 of night (18:00–06:00)**; one game hour is 40 seconds. Source: Liquipedia, "Daytime".
- At that clock, **13 nights is 13 × 8 min = 104 minutes** before the setup phase and any longer final night. Too long for a weeknight session of three; right in shape (long day, long night).
- The cycle is tunable in a map (the game-time scale, or a Lua periodic that sets the time of day), so the base clock is a reference, not a constraint.

## They Are Billions (verified, older sources)
- **One minute of real time is one game day** (developer blog, 2017). Survival runs are **80 / 100 / 120 days** (later 150); the developer said a longer game is easier because the colony has more time to build. Players call the late game "pure boredom" once the map is clean and the colony waits for the final wave. Pause is used constantly to think.
- Lesson for us: a fixed day count with a known final wave works, and the end must not be a wait. Our thirteen is short enough that the wait never comes if every night asks something.

## Age of Darkness: Final Stand (to verify at the editor desk)
- Verified: a day–night cycle with **Death Nights** at intervals, announced in advance; a **Malice** (random affliction) is placed on the player every Death Night, and surviving one offers a choice of three **Blessings**. Survival mode is "endlessly replayable" (no fixed end).
- From memory, unverified: a cycle of several minutes with the night the shorter half; Death Nights every few nights with a countdown on the HUD. **Confirm the cycle length and the Death Night interval by playing one run with a stopwatch.**
- Lesson for us: our event nights 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 are Death Nights by another name. Their Malice-then-Blessing loop is worth a look for the hard mode: an affliction on the event night, a boon for surviving it.

## The owner's instinct and the agent's recommendation
- Unseal "~40 minutes"; keep thirteen nights (sealed).
- Set the cycle from the walk, not from a wish: the day must hold a ritual strike (walk to the nearest ritual, kill the acolytes inside the bell's one-minute window, break the obelisk with siege, walk home) with a margin; the night must hold a wave that feels like Warcraft (90–120 seconds of fighting).
- First test values: **a 6-minute cycle, 4 of day and 2 of night** → 13 cycles ≈ 78 minutes, plus the council and a longer night 13: **about 90 minutes**. Fallback if that drags: 5 minutes (3.5 / 1.5) → about 70. Both are measured at the editor desk against the actual walk, which the terrain sets.
- The difficulty dial never touches the cycle (council, Game Designer seat).
